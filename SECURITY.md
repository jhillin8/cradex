# Security Policy

Take care when reporting a vulnerability: do not publish sensitive details or credentials in a public issue. Contact the Cradex maintainers privately with enough detail to reproduce the issue.

For background on the inherited sandboxing, approval, and network controls, see the upstream [Agent approvals & security](https://developers.openai.com/codex/agent-approvals-security) documentation. Behavior in Cradex may differ.

OpenAI's [Bugcrowd program](https://bugcrowd.com/engagements/openai) applies to OpenAI products; it is not a reporting channel for fork-specific issues.
